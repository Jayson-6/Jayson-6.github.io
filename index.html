Student Performance Analysis

An analysis of a public student records dataset to test three common assumptions about academic performance: whether study time, class absences, or past failures relate to a student's final grade.

Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables CORREL Function

Method: Used Excel's CORREL function to measure the statistical relationship between each factor and final grade (G3), then built a pivot table and chart to visualise the clearest result.

Findings:

  • Study time showed almost no relationship with final grade (correlation of 0.10) — more reported study hours did not reliably predict better results.
  • Absences also showed almost no relationship (correlation of 0.03).
  • Past class failures had a clear negative relationship with final grade (correlation of −0.36) — students with more prior failures consistently scored lower.
